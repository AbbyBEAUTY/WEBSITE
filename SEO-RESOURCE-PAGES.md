# Evergreen SEO resource pages

Abi Beauty should build a durable, interconnected reference library for buying and shipping beauty products to Honduras. This is a hub-and-spoke resource system, not a blog cadence. Every page needs a stable URL, visible review date, accurate evidence, and contextual links.

## Planned cluster

- Hub: shipping beauty products to Honduras
- Verified provider profile based on Abi's real experience
- Fair provider comparison with published methodology
- Educational product-import readiness assessment with a personalized task list
- Landed-cost explainer with transparent worked examples
- Restricted beauty items from current official sources
- Shipping timelines separated by journey stage
- Mistakes and troubleshooting without rule circumvention
- Focused FAQ linking to authoritative resources

Provider identity is blocked: the current site says `Shipito`, while user context also mentioned `ShipItTo`. Verify the official name, legal entity, and URL before publishing provider-specific pages, claims, logos, or affiliate links.

## Required page anatomy

Each page needs a precise title, unique description, canonical, single H1, direct answer, published/verified dates, authorship and experience context, current official sources, contextual cluster navigation, disclosures, limitations, owner, and next-review date. Label firsthand experience, researched claims, worked examples, and estimates distinctly.

Do not publish fabricated ratings, guessed fees, unverified superlatives, unsupported delivery guarantees, private shipment details, credentials, or advice that circumvents declarations or restrictions. Use `data/seo-page-inventory.csv` to track evidence and production status through preview QA and scheduled review.
