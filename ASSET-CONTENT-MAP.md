# Abi Beauty asset-to-content map

## Current position

The repository contains 104 non-empty visual assets; 28 are currently referenced by the MVP pages or shared stylesheet. The remaining library is expansion inventory, not a requirement to crowd every image into the launch pages.

Use an image only when it proves, demonstrates, or clarifies the page's answer. Do not create thin pages merely to consume assets.

## Visual families and intended page roles

### Firsthand product testing and reviews

Relevant families include mascara application, gloss application, cheek testing, concealer touch-up, mirror checks, swatches, vanity application, and beauty-counter aftermath.

Use for:

- individual product reviews;
- “who this is for / not for” sections;
- wear-test or application evidence;
- product-versus-product comparisons;
- category pages that route to substantive tests.

An image must not imply Abi tested a product unless that is true. Generated/editorial imagery must be labeled and must not substitute for firsthand evidence.

### Shade, skin, and product comparisons

Relevant families include friends comparing shades, foundation/concealer swatches, lip swatches, three-foundation swatches, and thoughtful/skeptical testing scenes.

Use for:

- comparison hubs;
- shade-selection education;
- skin-type or finish explainers;
- decision tools that assign a next research task.

### Honduras and logistics

Relevant families include Honduran vanity, Honduran courtyard, beauty workspace Honduras, parcel unboxing, package unboxing, and makeup-bag spreads.

Use for:

- buying-from-Honduras guides;
- provider and shipping resource pages;
- product-import readiness assessment;
- worked process examples and troubleshooting;
- local context that international beauty sites cannot provide.

### Routines and education

Relevant families include women getting ready, mature skincare ritual, serum/window ritual, fogged-mirror skincare, tropical window skincare, and vanity scenes.

Use for:

- beginner routines;
- skincare clusters;
- age/context-specific education;
- “what you actually need” qualification pages.

### Product/category atmosphere

Relevant families include raspberry still lifes, cream/powder textures, cosmetic smears, powder sweeps, poolside beauty, hibiscus, and decorative product cutouts.

Use as supporting visual language for category hubs, hero sections, social cards, or backgrounds. These do not provide product-test evidence by themselves.

## Reference images

The three `assets/reference/*.png` files are design-direction artifacts. They are not production content and should remain unreferenced unless deliberately converted into documentation outside the public payload.

## Page-production gate

Before a new page is approved, record:

1. Search intent and canonical owner.
2. Primary visual and what it proves.
3. Whether the visual is firsthand, generated/editorial, product-supplied, or licensed.
4. Supporting visual(s) and their purpose.
5. Alt text based on the visible information, not keyword stuffing.
6. Mobile crop and intrinsic dimensions.
7. Social-preview image.
8. Relevant UGC or original evidence still needed from Abi.

This map implements the image/video and original-content layer in `SEO-ARCHITECTURE.md`; it does not replace the need for a current keyword/SERP map or real Abi product evidence.
